Challenge #597

ASCII Badge

Your friend configured his Hackropole badge to encode a flag on the 8 LEDs. Taking advantage of him having his back turned, you brutally dump the firmware of his board.

Can you find the flag from the dump?

Note: Possessing the badge does not help solve the challenge, and the original firmware has not been modified.
